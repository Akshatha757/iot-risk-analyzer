IoT Security Blueprint

Best practices and architectural designs for securing home and enterprise smart environments.

Segmented IoT Network Topology

The golden rule of IoT safety is network segregation. Secure your infrastructure by implementing these architectural standards:

  1. Separate VLAN: Create a virtual LAN on your router specifically for devices that do not need access to local folders/servers.
  2. Guest Network Isolation: If VLANs are not supported, turn on a Guest Wi-Fi SSID and enable "Client Isolation" (prevents devices from scanning each other).
  3. Firewall Inbound Blocks: Configure the router firewall to block all WAN-to-LAN inbound traffic targeting IoT device IPs.

Credential & Access Hardening

Minimize exposure to scanners and automated brute force bots:

  • Never reuse passwords across smart home apps. If a provider database leaks, brute-force bots will crack your devices.
  • Disable UPnP (Universal Plug and Play) on both your home gateway router and device configs to stop automated remote port mappings.
  • Set up Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) on IoT vendor accounts to prevent credential-stuffing hijackings.

Secure Management Protocols

Protect management panels and diagnostic streams:

  • Disable obsolete unencrypted protocols (Telnet Port 23, FTP Port 21, HTTP Port 80) in favor of SSH (Port 22) and HTTPS (Port 443).
  • For security cameras, require strong passwords on RTSP feeds and utilize secure streaming wrappers when connecting remotely.
